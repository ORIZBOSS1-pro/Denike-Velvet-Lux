About Denike & The Velvet Lux Heritage

Founded by Denike, Denike Velvet Lux is a celebration of opulent African craftsmanship blended with modern minimalist luxury. Our mission is to craft fine jewelry pieces that do not just adorn, but tell a story of royalty, confidence, and enduring beauty.

From custom 18k solid gold wedding bands to richly strung traditional Edo and Yoruba coral beads, every piece is handcrafted with precision, authenticity, and non-tarnish materials tailored for the modern connoisseur.
